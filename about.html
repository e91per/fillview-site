About Fillview

Fillview is an independent software service built to turn the ten tabs a trader opens every day into one screen.

What we do

Different screens on every exchange, a journal that lives somewhere else, market data scattered across sites: traders spend more time checking than deciding. Fillview puts the chart at the center and brings positions, screening and journaling into one place.

Fillview never moves user funds. Exchange connections are read-only, and keys and records stay on the user's own device.
