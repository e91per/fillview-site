Questions

Is Fillview really free?

Charts, drawing, the calculator and the economic calendar are free with no sign-up. Pro features open with a free 3-day trial, then a monthly subscription paid in Telegram Stars.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Telegram, open Settings → My Stars and cancel the Fillview subscription. Pro stays on until the paid period ends, and your journal stays on your device.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser on your device. Download a backup file any time, or send it to your own Telegram.

Is this investment advice?

No. Fillview is a tool. Every trading decision and its result is your own.

Can I use my own Pine Script indicators?

Yes, with Pro. Fillview runs most indicator scripts — inputs, ta.* functions, request.security, arrays, boxes, lines, labels and tables. User-defined types, matrices and strategies aren’t supported yet; the editor tells you which line.
